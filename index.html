What you have

You now hold a full answer to a real examination question, built one decision at a time, with a line of argument that you can defend and a counter argument you have already answered.

The eight decisions in this capstone were all the same decision, asked eight ways: does this sentence argue, or does it only describe?

That is the whole difference between an answer in the middle bands and an answer in the top two. It is also the only thing you cannot fix in the examination room if you have not practised it beforehand.
